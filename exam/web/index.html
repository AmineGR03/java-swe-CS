How this works

  • The clock is authoritative. It is kept by the server, not the page. Closing the tab, reloading or stopping the page's JavaScript does not stop it.
  • At zero, you are submitted. Whatever was last autosaved is graded automatically. Your work is saved every couple of seconds.
  • No test results until you submit. During the attempt the only feedback is from javac — compile errors, nothing about correctness.
  • One submission. Submitting ends the part; you cannot reopen it.
  • No outside help. No internet, no AI, no IDE, no looking at tests/ or solutions/. The environment does not police your filesystem — the honest constraint is you.
